## Overview

The software engineering career is dynamic and offers multiple progression paths. This module covers everything from level progression to personal branding, helping you make informed decisions about your career trajectory.

## Career Levels

### Level Progression

| Level | Focus | Key Skills | Timeline |
|-------|-------|------------|----------|
| Junior | Learning & Growth | Technical fundamentals | 0-2 years |
| Mid-Level | Independence & Ownership | System design, mentoring | 2-5 years |
| Senior | Leadership & Impact | Architecture, strategy | 5-8 years |
| Staff | Technical Vision | Cross-team initiatives | 8-12 years |
| Principal | Organization-wide Impact | Business alignment | 12+ years |
| Fellow | Industry Impact | Innovation & Research | 15+ years |
