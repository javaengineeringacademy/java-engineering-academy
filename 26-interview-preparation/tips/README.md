# General Interview Tips

Master technical interviews with mindset, preparation, and communication strategies.

## Overview

Success in technical interviews requires more than technical knowledge. This guide covers mindset, preparation strategies, and communication techniques.

## Mindset and Attitude

### Growth Mindset
- **Believe in Improvement**: Skills can be developed through dedication
- **Embrace Challenges**: See difficult problems as learning opportunities
- **Learn from Criticism**: Use feedback to improve
- **Find Inspiration**: Learn from others' success

### Confidence Building
- **Prepare Thoroughly**: Knowledge builds confidence
- **Practice Out Loud**: Verbalize your thinking
- **Mock Interviews**: Simulate real conditions
- **Positive Self-Talk**: Replace "I can't" with "I'm learning"

### Handling Pressure
- **Breathe**: Deep breathing reduces anxiety
- **Pause**: Take time to think before answering
- **Reframe**: See interviews as conversations, not interrogations
- **Perspective**: One interview doesn't define your career

## Preparation Strategy

- [ ] Study distributed systems concepts
- [ ] Practice system design problems
- [ ] Learn about databases and caching

### Week 5-6: Behavioral
- [ ] Prepare STAR stories
- [ ] Research company values
- [ ] Practice communication

### Week 7-8: Mock Interviews
- [ ] Practice with peers
- [ ] Time yourself
- [ ] Get feedback

### Daily Practice
- **2-3 Coding Problems**: Mix of Easy/Medium
- **1 System Design**: Practice with timer
- **Behavioral Questions**: 2-3 STAR stories
- **Company Research**: 30 minutes daily

## Communication Techniques

### Think Out Loud
- **Explain Your Thought Process**: Show how you approach problems
- **Discuss Trade-offs**: Consider alternatives
- **Ask for Feedback**: Get interviewer input
- **Show Reasoning**: Demonstrate logical thinking

### Ask Clarifying Questions
**Before Starting:**
- "Can the input be empty?"
- "Are there negative numbers?"
- "What's the expected output?"
- "Are there performance requirements?"

**During Implementation:**
- "Should I handle this edge case?"
- "Is this the approach you're looking for?"
- "Should I optimize further?"

### Handle Hints
- **Listen Carefully**: Interviewer hints are valuable
- **Adapt Your Approach**: Be flexible
- **Show Learning**: Demonstrate ability to learn
- **Don't Ignore Hints**: They're guiding you

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Coding
- **Not Testing Edge Cases**: Always consider empty, null, large inputs
- **Poor Time/Space Complexity**: Analyze before coding
- **Not Asking Clarifying Questions**: Understand the problem first
- **Rushing to Code**: Plan before implementing
- **Ignoring Code Quality**: Use meaningful names, clean structure

### System Design
- **Not Discussing Trade-offs**: Every decision has pros/cons
- **Over-Engineeringing Solutions**: Keep it simple
- **Ignoring Scalability Considerations**: Think about growth
- **Not Considering Failure Modes**: Plan for failures
- **Forgetting About Monitoring**: Observability matters

### Behavioral
- **Not Using STAR Method**: Structure your answers
- **Being Too Vague**: Use specific examples
- **Blaming Others**: Take ownership
- **Not Showing Growth**: Demonstrate learning
- **Using Same Story for Every Question**: Have variety

## Interview Day Tips

### Before the Interview
- **Get Good Sleep**: Rest is crucial for performance
- **Eat Well**: Avoid heavy meals
- **Arrive Early**: 10-15 minutes buffer
- **Review Notes**: Quick refresh of key points
- **Positive Visualization**: Imagine success

### During the Interview
- **Listen Carefully**: Understand the question fully
- **Take Notes**: Write down key requirements
- **Think Before Coding**: Plan your approach
- **Test Your Solution**: Walk through examples
- **Communicate**: Keep interviewer informed

### After the Interview
- **Send Thank You**: Email within 24 hours
- **Reflect**: What went well, what to improve
- **Follow Up**: If no response in 1 week
- **Keep Practicing**: Continue improving

## Company Research

### Before Interview
- **Company Mission**: What do they value?
- **Recent News**: What are they working on?
- **Tech Stack**: What technologies do they use?
- **Interview Process**: What to expect?

### Questions to Ask
- "What's the team's biggest challenge right now?"
- "How do you approach technical decisions?"
- "What does success look like in this role?"
- "What's the onboarding process like?"

## Stress Management

### Physical Techniques
- **Deep Breathing**: 4-7-8 technique
- **Progressive Relaxation**: Tense and release muscles
- **Power Pose**: Stand tall before interview
- **Movement**: Walk or stretch before interview

### Mental Techniques
- **Positive Affirmations**: "I am prepared and capable"
- **Visualization**: Imagine successful interview
- **Reframe Anxiety**: See excitement, not fear
- **Focus on Process**: Not outcome

## After the Interview

### If You Get the Offer
- **Celebrate**: Acknowledge your achievement
- **Evaluate**: Consider fit, growth, compensation
- **Negotiate**: Research market rates
- **Decide**: Make informed choice

### If You Don't Get the Offer
- **Don't Dwell**: It's not personal
- **Learn**: Request feedback if possible
- **Improve**: Work on weak areas
- **Keep Going**: Apply to more companies

### Continuous Improvement
- **Track Progress**: Monitor improvement over time
- **Adjust Strategy**: Focus on weak areas
- **Seek Feedback**: From mentors and peers
- **Stay Current**: Keep up with industry trends
