# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Java Engineering Academy a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback with care.
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private security
advisory when appropriate or by contacting the maintainers through the repository owner profile. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including a warning, temporary ban, permanent ban, or removal
from project spaces. Enforcement decisions are based on the impact of behavior on the community, not solely on intent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public
spaces.
