# Exam Preparation Strategies

## Before the Exam
1. Read the exam guide - Understand domains and weights
2. Create a study plan - Allocate time per domain
3. Practice hands-on - Labs and real environments
4. Take practice exams - Identify weak areas
5. Review official documentation

## Study Strategies
1. Active learning - Do not just read, do
2. Spaced repetition - Review at intervals
3. Teach others - Explain concepts
4. Practice under pressure - Timed exams
5. Focus on weak areas

## During the Exam
1. Read questions carefully - Watch for keywords
2. Eliminate wrong answers
3. Time management
4. Flag and return to uncertain answers
5. Stay calm

## After the Exam
1. Review results - Understand mistakes
2. Document gaps
3. Share knowledge
4. Keep learning

## Time Management
- 2-3 months for Associate level
- 3-6 months for Professional level
- 1-2 hours daily study
- Weekends for hands-on practice
